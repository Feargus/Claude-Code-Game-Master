# Social skills

**Load for:** convincing, threatening, lying, fitting in.

The anchor is in **bold** and is always one of the three dice. The other two follow the description of the action; the applications below are examples, not a closed list. Attributes are always written in the order STR, AGI, DFT, VIG, LOG, ITU, NRV, PRS, EMP.

## Persuasion

*Anchor: PRS · Status: canon*

Arguing, haggling, negotiating, seducing.

- Argument: LOG, **PRS**, EMP
- Haggling: LOG, ITU, **PRS**
- Seduction: **PRS**, EMP, EMP
- Rallying a crowd: **PRS**, PRS, EMP

## Intimidation

*Anchor: PRS · Status: canon*

Threats, pressure, commands, shouting someone down.

- Open threat: NRV, **PRS**, PRS
- Looming: STR, STR, **PRS**
- Quiet, specific threat: LOG, **PRS**, EMP
- Interrogation by pressure: NRV, **PRS**, EMP
- Shouting someone down: VIG, **PRS**, PRS

## Streetwise

*Anchor: PRS · Status: canon*

Gangs, fences, codes, who to ask and how. On the street, how you carry yourself decides whether you get answers or a knife.

- Finding a fence or dealer: LOG, **PRS**, EMP
- Recalling a gang's history and colors: LOG, ITU, **PRS**
- Avoiding a deadly faux pas: ITU, NRV, **PRS**
- Spreading or gathering rumors: ITU, **PRS**, EMP
- Not looking like a mark: NRV, **PRS**, PRS

## Deception

*Anchor: EMP · Status: canon*

Lies, cons, acting, disguise.

- Smooth lie: ITU, NRV, **EMP**
- Long con: LOG, PRS, **EMP**
- Acting a role: NRV, PRS, **EMP**
- Disguise: DFT, ITU, **EMP**

## Corpspeak

*Anchor: EMP · Status: canon*

Corporate protocol, bureaucracy, high society, and looking like you belong there, mostly by mirroring the people around you.

- Navigating a bureaucracy: LOG, LOG, **EMP**
- Fitting in at a gala: ITU, PRS, **EMP**
- Knowing who's who in a corp: LOG, ITU, **EMP**
- Getting past a receptionist: NRV, PRS, **EMP**
