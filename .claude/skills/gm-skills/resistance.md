# Resistance skills

**Load for:** the body or mind must hold out: poison, pain, fear, interrogation.

The anchor is in **bold** and is always one of the three dice. The other two follow the description of the action; the applications below are examples, not a closed list. Attributes are always written in the order STR, AGI, DFT, VIG, LOG, ITU, NRV, PRS, EMP.

## Endurance

*Anchor: VIG · Status: canon*

Resisting poison, disease, drugs, exhaustion, shock, pain, heat and cold.

- Resisting a toxin: **VIG**, VIG, NRV
- Forced march, no sleep: STR, **VIG**, NRV
- Staying conscious after a hit: **VIG**, NRV, NRV
- Drinking and staying sharp: **VIG**, VIG, LOG

## Composure

*Anchor: NRV · Status: canon*

Resisting fear, torture, interrogation, temptation and manipulation.

- Staying calm under fire: ITU, **NRV**, NRV
- Resisting interrogation: VIG, **NRV**, NRV
- Noticing you're being manipulated: ITU, **NRV**, EMP
- Resisting temptation: LOG, ITU, **NRV**
- Solitary confinement: LOG, **NRV**, NRV
