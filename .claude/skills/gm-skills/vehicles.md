# Vehicles skills

**Load for:** driving, flying, chases, drones. **Untrained:** −1.

The anchor is in **bold** and is always one of the three dice. The other two follow the description of the action; the applications below are examples, not a closed list. Attributes are always written in the order STR, AGI, DFT, VIG, LOG, ITU, NRV, PRS, EMP.

## Rigging

*Anchor: AGI · Untrained: −1 · Status: canon*

Jacked into a vehicle or drone, steering by muscle memory and proprioception: its sensors become your senses and its body yours, while your own body fades. Requires a control rig. A simple command to a drone ("move there") needs no roll; steering by hand is Piloting.

- Combat driving, jacked in: **AGI**, ITU, NRV
- Reading the machine's senses (radar, thermal): **AGI**, LOG, ITU
- Keeping hold of your own body, jumping back out: **AGI**, VIG, NRV

## Piloting

*Anchor: DFT · Untrained: −1 · Status: canon*

Anything with controls, steered the normal way: wheel, stick, remote control. Specialization comes from traits (e.g. +2, aircraft only).

- High-speed chase: **DFT**, ITU, NRV
- Precision maneuver (a tight alley, landing on a roof): AGI, **DFT**, ITU
- Long haul: **DFT**, VIG, NRV
- Ramming: STR, **DFT**, NRV
