# Knowledge skills

**Load for:** knowing and planning: science, tactics, improvised fields of knowledge.

The anchor is in **bold** and is always one of the three dice. The other two follow the description of the action; the applications below are examples, not a closed list. Attributes are always written in the order STR, AGI, DFT, VIG, LOG, ITU, NRV, PRS, EMP.

## Science

*Anchor: LOG · Status: canon*

Chemistry, biology, physics, engineering theory.

- Recalling: **LOG**, LOG, ITU
- Analyzing a sample: DFT, **LOG**, LOG
- Improvising a chemical: DFT, **LOG**, ITU

## Tactics

*Anchor: LOG · Status: canon*

Successful fighters make sure of victory first and only then seek battle; losers fight first and then look for a way to win. Tactics is planning, positions, timing and reading the flow of a fight. A Tactics roll before contact (choosing positions, setting an ambush, picking the moment) changes the situation, and with it the edge order in the first beats. During a fight it reads the flow and leads a team.

- Planning an ambush or assault: **LOG**, LOG, ITU
- Choosing positions before contact: AGI, **LOG**, ITU
- Reading a fight as it happens: **LOG**, ITU, NRV
- Leading under fire: **LOG**, NRV, PRS
